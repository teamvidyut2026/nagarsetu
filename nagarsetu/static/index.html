The AI proposes. The kernel decides.

Language models are excellent at reading a messy Hinglish complaint and a blurry photo. They should never be the thing that decides whether a live wire outside a school waits 12 hours or 48, or whether a ticket is closed. NagarSetu splits those jobs.

01

Citizen reports

Text, voice or photo, in Hindi, English or Hinglish, from a phone. Location pin snaps to a ward.

02

AI proposes

Claude extracts category, severity, hazards and a bilingual summary, and flags spam or prompt-injection. Output is a schema-checked proposal.

03

Kernel decides

Deterministic rules from policy.json: hazard floors, SLA, department, human-review gates, duplicate merge.

04

Crew fixes

Least-loaded crew in the zone gets it. "Done" needs geotagged proof near the site.

05

Citizen closes

Only the citizen confirms or reopens (auto-close after 48 h). Reopen escalates. Breaches climb the ladder.
